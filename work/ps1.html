Concrete supply — own batching plant vs RMC

1. Grade-wise concrete requirement

GradeShareQuantity (m³)
M3560%7,200
M3020%2,400
M2520%2,400
Total100%12,000

Basis

  • Project duration 15 months × 25 working days = 300 working days; average production 12,000 ÷ 300 = 40 m³/day
  • Rates: cement ₹400/bag, FA ₹850/t, CA ₹1,100/t, admixture ₹70/kg
  • Admixture: superplasticiser at 1% of cement mass — suited to high-performance, ready-mixed and pumped concrete

Why the group recommends its own plant: the 12,000 m³ is enough to use a plant fully, concreting runs over a long period, the total stays lower even after plant, pumps, labour, overheads and profit, and production, quality and pouring continuity stay under the site team's control without depending on RMC deliveries through traffic.

Review notes — checks on the PS1 figures

  • Materials, RMC supply, RMC labour (9 × ₹1,050 + 18 × ₹850 for 300 days = ₹74,25,000) and both subtotals all check out ✓
  • The deck shows own-plant profit as ₹34,33,032 (5% on subtotal + overhead), but its final ₹7.19 crore uses 5% + 5% on the subtotal — corrected above to ₹32,69,554 so the lines add up; RMC uses the same simple method
  • Labour is inconsistent: the labour slide costs 33 workers over 375 days (₹1,11,18,750) while stating 15 × 25 = 300 working days (₹88,95,000), and the plant table uses ₹1,33,35,000 — neither figure verify
  • The recommendation text says concreting spans about 12 months, against the 15-month duration used elsewhere
  • The M35 ratio is written as “00:05.5” (the quantities match 1 : 0.5 : 1) — recalculate from an approved mix design
  • The basis of “transit mixers (2 mixers / month) ₹2,50,000” is unclear
  • The comparison is a preliminary academic exercise, not a confirmed saving

Conclusion

PS1 sets the baseline for every later sheet: an 8-acre, seven-block mall with three parking levels, about 12,000 m³ of concrete and a 15-month (300 working-day) programme at 40 m³ a day. The layout groups the offices at the main gate and the yards, stores, test lab and MEP room in one strip, and the concrete study recommends an own batching plant at about ₹7.19 crore against ₹9.05 crore for RMC — once the labour figure is made consistent.
